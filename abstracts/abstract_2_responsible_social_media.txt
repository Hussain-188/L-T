Title: FocusFeed — A Responsible Social Media Platform with User-Controlled Interest Modes, Collaborative Content Creation, and AI-Powered Safety Governance

Abstract:

Mainstream social media platforms optimize for engagement duration rather than user intent, producing feeds cluttered with algorithmic provocations, undisclosed promotions, and content bearing no relation to what the viewer actually sought. Users cannot meaningfully steer what they see, cannot distinguish human-authored posts from machine-generated material, and have no practical way to collaborate on shared creative projects without exposing themselves to harassment or manipulation. FocusFeed is a proof-of-concept social platform built around a fundamentally different premise: the user declares what they want to accomplish right now, and the system organizes itself around that declaration.

The platform introduces two complementary control mechanisms. Interest Modes let a user select, combine, and weight topic dimensions — science, sport, technology, art, personal narratives, social causes — so the feed reflects genuine preference rather than inferred behavioral profiles. Focus Modes layer a temporal intent on top: learning mode surfaces explanatory and long-form material, discovery mode broadens the horizon toward unfamiliar perspectives, and creation mode opens collaborative workspaces where multiple contributors can propose, review, stitch, and publish combined media under moderator governance with explicit contributor consent.

Safety and trust are handled through five integrated subsystems operating in concert. A deep-learning classifier labels AI-generated or AI-altered images, video, and text at upload time, attaching visible provenance tags before any content enters the feed. A content moderation pipeline screens for harassment, graphic material, and verifiable misinformation, routing borderline cases to community moderators rather than suppressing them silently. A behavioral analysis module detects bot networks and artificially inflated engagement by examining account creation patterns, interaction velocity, and coordinated posting signatures. Child-safety controls enforce age-gated content boundaries and restrict direct messaging from unverified accounts. Finally, a transparency layer lets every user inspect why a particular post appeared in their feed, report content with structured reasoning, and appeal any moderation decision.

FocusFeed demonstrates that user control, content safety, and meaningful discovery can coexist within a single coherent architecture, without relying on opaque recommendation algorithms or pervasive behavioral surveillance.
